About CBC MASTER Privacy

CBC MASTER follows a local-first approach. Teaching records are kept in the browser's local storage unless you explicitly export a backup.

Do not store unnecessary sensitive information. Keep your device protected with a secure screen lock and avoid sharing exported backups.

CBC MASTER does not require third-party advertising or analytics services.
