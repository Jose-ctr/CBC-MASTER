Privacy & Security

Local only

CBC MASTER is designed to keep your teaching workspace on this device. Student and teacher records are not automatically uploaded to a cloud service.

✓
Local-first storage Your workspace is stored in this browser on this device.
✓
No advertising trackers CBC MASTER does not require third-party advertising trackers.
✓
No secret data transmission The app does not automatically send your student records to a remote server.
✓
Export and delete controls You control when your local workspace is backed up or deleted.

About CBC MASTER Privacy

CBC MASTER V2 is being built as a local-first teacher productivity tool. The application does not need an account or cloud database for its core workspace.

For deployed versions, use HTTPS and keep the application and dependencies updated. Browser storage is not the same as encrypted storage, so protect access to the device and browser profile.

Privacy principle Your classroom records should remain under your control.
